# **App Name**: KrishiMargdarshak

## Core Features:

- AI-Powered Crop Advisory: Provide farmers with real-time, location-specific, actionable advice, leveraging a tool that reasons over available crop and weather data to recommend optimal farming practices.
- Image-Based Pest/Disease Detection: Allow farmers to upload images of crops for AI-powered diagnosis of pests and diseases.
- Multilingual Support: Enable the platform to support multiple Indian languages.
- Voice Input/Output: Offer voice input and output options for low-literacy users.
- Weather-Based Alerts: Send weather-based alerts and predictive insights to farmers, informing them of changing conditions.
- Real-Time Market Price Tracking: Provide farmers with up-to-date market prices for their crops.
- Feedback Form: Gather user feedback for continuous improvement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy Green (#6A994E), reflecting agriculture and nature.
- Background color: Light Beige (#F2E8CF), providing a soft and natural feel.
- Accent color: Terracotta (#BC6C25), for calls-to-action and important highlights.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif that combines a modern look and a little warmth or personality.
- Use simple, intuitive icons to represent features and information. Prioritize clarity and ease of understanding, and use local imager to provide familiarity.
- Prioritize a clean, farmer-friendly, and minimalistic layout, ensuring the app is easy to navigate and understand even for users with low digital literacy.
- Incorporate smooth, subtle animations for navigation and transitions to enhance user experience.
